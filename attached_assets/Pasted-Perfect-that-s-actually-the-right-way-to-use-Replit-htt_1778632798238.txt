# ERRANDLY — FULL PRODUCT OVERVIEW

## Product Summary

Errandly is a trusted hyperlocal marketplace that connects people who need everyday tasks completed (“Customers”) with verified local individuals willing to carry out those tasks (“Runners”) for payment.

The platform is designed for urban communities where people often need help with tasks such as picking up items, delivering packages, buying groceries, waiting in line, handling physical paperwork, or running location-specific errands.

Errandly prioritizes safety, trust, and accountability by combining strong identity verification, escrow payments, real-time tracking, dispute resolution, and user ratings.

The system should feel like a secure blend of:

* gig marketplace
* local delivery platform
* trusted personal assistant network
* neighborhood task ecosystem

---

# Core Vision

Enable anyone to outsource physical errands to trusted nearby people quickly and safely.

---

## 1. Customers
