Privacy Policy

At Errand, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information as you help and get help within your community.

On this page

  • Data Collection
  • How We Use Your Info
  • Escrow & Payment Security
  • Your Rights

Data Collection

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, post an errand, or communicate with other users. This includes your name, email address, phone number, and professional credentials if you are applying as a verified "Errander."

Additionally, we collect geolocation data to facilitate hyperlocal task matching. This allows us to show you errands within your immediate Uyo community or your specified city limits.

  • Identity Information (Full name, ID verification documents)
  • Contact Information (Address, Email, Phone)
  • Usage Data (IP address, device type, app interaction logs)

How We Use Your Info

Our primary goal in collecting information is to provide a safe and efficient marketplace. We use your data to:

  • Facilitate the matching of Task Posters with qualified Erranders.
  • Verify the identity of users to maintain community trust and safety.
  • Process payments and manage the automated escrow system.
  • Improve our logistics algorithms for better route planning.
  • Provide customer support and resolve disputes between users.

Escrow & Payment Security

shield

Your financial security is our top priority.

All financial transactions on Errand are processed through our secure Escrow platform. We do not store your full credit card details or bank login credentials on our servers. Instead, we use industry-leading payment processors that comply with PCI-DSS standards.

Funds are held in a secure vault and only released to the Errander once the Task Poster confirms the errand has been completed to their satisfaction. This "Hold-and-Release" mechanism ensures that your money is protected until the job is done.

Your Rights

You have full control over your personal data on Errand. Under our privacy framework, you are entitled to the following:

Access & Portability

Request a copy of all data we hold about you in a machine-readable format.

The Right to Erasure

Request that we delete your account and all associated personal records.

Correction

Update or fix any inaccurate personal information at any time via your profile settings.

Opt-Out

Unsubscribe from non-essential marketing communications and data tracking.

Have questions about your privacy?

Our legal and safety team is here to help you understand how we protect your information. Reach out anytime.
